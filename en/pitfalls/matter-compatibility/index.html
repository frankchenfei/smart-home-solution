Pitfall 6: ecosystem compatibility and lock-in

The costliest trap is not a broken device. It is a house full of perfectly working devices you cannot take anywhere else.

Three disciplines: critical devices must support Matter or a local API, at least two independent hub paths, you must own the accounts and the devices. Then no vendor policy change can paralyse your home.

What Matter solves#

  • Cross-ecosystem control: one light can be managed by Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa and a vendor app simultaneously.
  • Multi-admin: no re-pairing when you change ecosystems.
  • Standardised local communication on the LAN.

What Matter does not solve#

ScenarioReality
Multi-gang key customisationMostly vendor-specific channels
Complex lighting scenes and effectsLimited scene capability in Matter
Curtain calibration and percentagesInconsistent across ecosystems
Advanced sensor parametersOften trimmed
Firmware updatesStill vendor app dependent

Matter guarantees that something can be controlled, not that every feature works.

Building redundancy#

  • Hub layer: a vendor hub plus a self-hosted local hub, or two different ecosystems.
  • Execution layer: lighting and curtains that support Matter or a local API.
  • Interaction layer: more than one voice assistant; at least two apps from different ecosystems.
  • Never keep all automations in the cloud only.

Accounts and ownership#

  • Register with the owner's own phone number and email, not the installer's account.
  • Keep pairing codes and QR photos.
  • Record brand, model, serial number and room for every device.
  • On handover, confirm admin rights are transferred, in writing.

Moving and second-hand devices#

  • Moving home: devices on open standards or re-pairable ones travel well; built-in switch panels depend on the model.
  • Second-hand: some vendors require the previous owner to unpair in the app first. Always confirm before buying.
  • Selling a property: disclose the smart system as an asset and complete an account and permission handover.

Four clauses for the contract#

  1. Device list (brand, model, serial) and accounts belong to the owner.
  2. The provider must not retain exclusive backend admin access.
  3. A list of functions available when the internet is down.
  4. A data and configuration export plan if the provider ceases service.

If every device comes from one vendor, works only in that vendor's app, and exposes no local interface, treat it as a five-year rental rather than a purchase.

Still have questions?

Can Apple Home, Xiaomi and Google Home coexist?

Yes, where devices support Matter or the vendor provides bridges. Note that the same device may appear differently in each ecosystem, so designate one as primary and use the others as supplements.

Are all Matter devices locally controlled?

The specification supports local communication, but implementations vary. Test it — disconnect the internet and check whether the app still works.

How do I avoid being locked in?

Three things: critical devices on open standards, redundant hub paths, and ownership of accounts and devices written into the contract.
