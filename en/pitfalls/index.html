Six pitfalls, ordered by cost#

  1. Missing neutral wires — demolition and rework, from ¥3,000 per home.
  2. Wrong hub or gateway — offline paralysis, cross-gateway scenes failing, whole device batches wasted.
  3. Insufficient curtain provision — surface trunking, exposed tracks, no double track.
  4. Service box too small and too hot — chronic network dropouts, very expensive to diagnose.
  5. Wi-Fi roaming and dead zones — devices offline, slow voice, a broken experience.
  6. Ecosystem lock-in — one price rise or service shutdown affects the whole home.

The principle is not to buy the most expensive option everywhere, but to never compromise on things that cannot be redone.

Still have questions?

Can these be fixed afterwards?

Neutral wires, curtain power and network points generally require damaging finishes. Hub, roaming and compatibility issues can usually be resolved by replacing devices or reconfiguring.

How do I judge whether a provider is professional?

Look for a point plan, an acceptance-tested scene table, written acceptance criteria, and straight answers about which functions stop working offline.
