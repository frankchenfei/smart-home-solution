A quick call or message with your area, layout and construction stage is the fastest start — we can share an initial point plan before arranging a survey.

What to prepare#

  1. A floor plan (CAD or a clear photo is fine).
  2. Construction stage: not started, electrical in progress, fit-out complete, or already living there.
  3. A budget range or expectation, if you have one.

What you receive#

  • Point plan — every switch, network point, sensor and curtain power location.
  • Device list — brand, model, protocol, and whether local control is supported.
  • Scene table — triggers and actions for arrive, leave, movie, sleep and night.
  • Three-tier quotation — entry, practical and premium, with the cannot-be-redone items marked.

Still have questions?

Can you quote without producing a plan?

No, and we would advise against it. A price without a point plan and circuit count is meaningless and always becomes an add-on later.

Do you charge for the plan?

Surveys and plans are free in our direct-operation cities. For remote projects, design review is free and site attendance is charged at travel cost.
