Five quick checks#

  1. Ceiling height — European Classic wants 2.9 m or more; Cream and Japandi are the most forgiving.
  2. Daylight — avoid dark timber and grey tones in dim homes; favour Cream and French Luxe.
  3. Cleaning cost — battens, mouldings and chandeliers look superb and are hard to maintain; Modern Minimal and Cream are the easiest.
  4. Smart compatibility — the more no-main-light, cove lighting and electric curtains a style uses, the more electrical and point planning it needs.
  5. Furniture budget — European and French furniture typically costs 1.5–2.5 times Modern Minimal.

Still have questions?

Which style ages best?

Modern Minimal and Japandi endure because they rely on proportion and material rather than trends. Cream and French Luxe lean more on soft furnishing, which can be refreshed after three years.

Does the smart system differ much between styles?

Yes. More lighting layers, coves and electric curtains demand more dimming capability and more points, so produce the point plan at render stage.

With a tight budget, which features should I protect?

Lighting and colour, which account for about 70% of the perceived quality. Panelling, mouldings and stone can be reduced or applied selectively.
