AI generation takes ten seconds, but the experience depends on the point plan, scene table and device list. Bundling renders, construction drawings and smart points together is cheaper and far less stressful than hiring three separate parties.

Four steps#

  1. Briefing (1 day) — area, layout, construction stage, household routines and budget. On-site surveys in Shenzhen; remote review for Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan and other cities.
  2. AI generation (10 seconds, delivered in 3 days) — 8–12 renders, three colour schemes and a material list by area and style, fixing the aesthetic direction.
  3. Scheme development (3–5 working days) — translating the render into buildable documents: construction drawings, point plan, scene table, device list and quotation.
  4. Construction and commissioning (following the programme) — electrical and carpentry briefings, installation, scene commissioning, user training and acceptance.

Acceptance criteria you can put in the contract#

  • Renders: all main spaces covered, including night views and lighting switched on.
  • Point plan: switches, network, curtain power, sensors and AP positions marked.
  • Scene table: arrive, leave, movie, sleep and night, with triggers and actions.
  • Acceptance: panel response ≤ 0.5 s, voice ≤ 1.5 s, ≥ −65 dBm Wi-Fi in every room, lighting and curtains still working offline.

Never buy renders alone. A render without a point plan and scene table gets diluted on site — this is the single biggest cause of rework.

Renders first or quotation first?

Renders first. Fixing the style and material grade before quoting avoids a vague estimate followed by repeated adjustments.

How do you deliver outside Shenzhen?

Remote design review plus certified local partners, with our engineers attending the electrical briefing and commissioning milestones remotely or on site.

What if I do not like the renders?

The package includes three revision rounds. If you are still unsatisfied you can stop before scheme development, paying only the render cost already incurred.
