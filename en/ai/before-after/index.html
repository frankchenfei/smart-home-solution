From shell to render, money goes into four things: electrical and points, wall and floor finishes, lighting and ceilings, furniture and soft furnishing. Fixing their grade at render stage is far cheaper than adding it mid-build.

What the comparison settles#

  • Walls: emulsion, decorative plaster or panelling? Up to six times the cost.
  • Floors: tile, timber or microcement? Affects feel and cleaning effort.
  • Lighting: is there a ceiling with coves? This creates the light layers you see in the render.
  • Points: switches, sockets, network and curtain power — impossible to change after the wall closes.

Three rooms, three priorities#

Living room

At shell stage confirm: TV wall socket height and count, sockets either side of the sofa, curtain box power, whether the ceiling reserves a cove. A no-main-light design needs at least three lighting circuits, decided during the electrical stage.

Master bedroom

Two-way switches and sockets both sides of the bed, wardrobe door swing and internal lighting, night light power. Lighting layers matter more than in the living room for sleep quality — keep everything at 2700–3000 K.

Dining room

Table-to-pendant relationship, sideboard sockets for coffee machines and kettles, and the serving route from the kitchen. Pendants usually sit 65–75 cm above the table, which a render shows immediately.

The most common rework is "we love the render but there is no power where it needs to be": curtain boxes, coves, WC sockets, camera supplies. Treat the render as a point checklist — tick every line before the wall closes.

Still have questions?

Can a second-hand apartment achieve the render?

Mostly yes. Limits are ceiling height, structural walls and electrical capacity; for older homes we solve power and network first and use wireless solutions for the smart layer.

Is the shell image a real photo?

In this demo the shell view is generated programmatically. In delivery we work from your own site photos and measured dimensions.

How is the renovation budget estimated?

Area × style factor for hard fit-out and materials (¥2,200–3,600 per m²) plus ¥140–220 per m² for the smart system. The detailed quotation follows the confirmed point plan.
