    The real value of a render is not that it looks good — it is that the whole family agrees on one aesthetic before any money is spent. Trying styles with AI typically saves one or two rounds of rework and tens of thousands in trial and error.

    Why start with an AI render#

    • Cheaper decisions: the same budget feels completely different in New Chinese, Modern Minimal or Cream style. A picture is ten times faster than a paragraph.
    • Family alignment: send the result to the family group chat instead of trying to describe "that premium feeling".
    • Budget lock-in: once the style is fixed, the grade of finishes, materials and furniture is largely fixed too, which stops mid-project increases.
    • Room for smart systems: every style implies different lighting and curtain details, so planning smart points at render stage prevents surface-mounted retrofits later.

    How the one-click flow works#

    1. Choose the home — 300 m² villa, 140 m² large flat, 120 m² four-bedroom or 90 m² three-bedroom. The engine adapts proportions and furniture scale to the area.
    2. Choose the style — six mainstream directions, all in one pass.
    3. Choose the room — living room, master bedroom, dining room, study or courtyard, so the whole home stays consistent.
    4. Generate and refine — agree the overall feeling first, then let a designer adjust points, furniture sizes and lighting layers to your routines.

    From render to reality#

    An AI render settles the aesthetic. Delivery needs three documents: a point plan (switches, network, curtain power, sensors), a scene table (arrive, leave, movie, sleep, night) and a device list (brand, model, whether local control is supported). With all three, the light in the render can actually appear in the house.

    Generate repeatedly. Run all six styles for the same layout, then vote with your family. Most clients settle the direction after the third or fourth generation.

    Still have questions?

    Can I build directly from an AI render?

    The render sets the direction and the shared aesthetic. Construction still needs a designer to develop the point plan, construction drawings and material list. Our package delivers both together.

    Do I need to upload a floor plan?

    You can generate from area and layout alone, but uploading a floor plan (CAD or a clear photo) improves accuracy for room widths, ceiling height and structural walls.

    What does a render package cost?

    The AI render package starts at ¥3,800 for 8–12 4K renders, three colour schemes and a material list, including three revision rounds. The full design package, including construction drawings and smart points, starts at ¥28,000.

    Can the style be changed after generating?

    Yes. Choose the direction first, then have a designer adjust palette intensity, material grades and furniture sizes until it matches what you want to see.

    Must the smart system be done at the same time?

    It is not mandatory but strongly recommended. Once the render is fixed, the lighting and curtain design is fixed too — which is the cheapest moment to reserve neutral wires, power and network points.
