Why we publish our cases#

Information asymmetry is the biggest cost in renovation. We publish the area, style, device schedule, scene table and cost breakdown of real projects so you can compare, specify and challenge quotations with evidence.

How to use these cases#

  1. Start with a similar area: 68-95 m2 for compact and cross-border projects, 110-140 m2 for family upgrades, 180 m2 and above for villas and townhouses.
  2. Then check style and tier: within one area, the style factor alone can create a 1.0-1.6x difference in cost.
  3. Then copy the scene table: the scene table is the part you can lift directly. Send it to any contractor and it beats describing what you want.

Every case page lists a plan ID and a cost breakdown. If a quotation differs by more than 30%, ask whether the difference is device grade, circuit count or site conditions.

Still have questions?

Are these real projects?

Yes. The data comes from projects delivered between 2016 and 2026, with privacy treatment (no client names or addresses). Prices are based on settled project costs including devices and labour.

What if my layout is not listed?

Use the AI render studio with your own area and layout, or contact us for a point plan and quotation within three working days.

Do I need the full smart package shown?

No. Prioritise what cannot be redone (network, points, curtain power) and phase the lighting and sensors. The schedules can be trimmed by priority.
