Brief and pain points#

A family of three, with the owner working from home. Beijing is dry in winter with strong western sun in summer; they wanted the home to be comfortable the moment they arrive.

  • Afternoon sun made the living room hotter than outside, with the AC running flat out.
  • The humidifier was often forgotten, leaving dry throats in the morning.
  • Study and living room shared one network; video calls stalled when others streamed.

What we did#

  1. Climate, shading and AC linkage: Living room curtains close automatically during peak sun; humidifier starts below 35% humidity
  2. Dedicated study network and quiet mode: Separate network point; meeting mode raises lighting to 4000 K and mutes alerts
  3. Layered lighting for the French look: Wall lights, coves and table lamps; the main light only when needed
  4. Dining detail: Pendant 68 cm above the table, sideboard sockets for coffee machine and kettle
  5. One-touch leave and security: Windows and curtains close, security arms, gas and water alarms announced

What is worth borrowing from this project#

  • In northern cities, climate linkage and shading matter more than mood lighting.
  • Anyone working from home needs a wired network point; wireless meetings are unreliable.
  • Reserve 15% of a French Luxe budget for light fittings and hardware, that is where the look comes from.

Still have questions?

What was the total budget?

Hard fit-out and materials about CNY 44.8-57.3 (10k), smart system about CNY 2.5 (10k), design and renders about CNY 2.8 (10k): a total of roughly CNY 47.7-60.2 (10k) excluding furniture and appliances.

Can the smart system be phased?

Yes. Complete the items that cannot be redone during construction (network points, neutral wires, curtain power, service box), then add sensors and automations three to six months after moving in.

Can you deliver this in my city?

We operate directly in Shenzhen, Shanghai, Beijing, Hangzhou, Chengdu and Suzhou. Other cities, including Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan, are delivered through remote design review with certified partners, with our engineers at the critical milestones.
