Still have questions?

What partnership models do you offer?

Three: project delivery (we design and deliver), product and technical support (you deliver, we back you up), and co-branding (jointly operating a local market and its after-sales).

We are new to smart home. Can we still partner?

Yes. You receive sales scripts, solution templates, installation standards, acceptance checklists and team training, with two to three on-site mentoring sessions in the first year.

How does territory protection work?

Authorisation is by city or district. Project cases and leads inside your territory belong to you, and we do not sell direct in that area.
