Property developers: standardised fit-out at scale

The key word in developer smart home is consistency: unit 1 and unit 500 must deliver the same experience.

Success rests on three things: a locked configuration list (no design freedom per unit), a batch commissioning workflow (configuration templates per building), and acceptance documentation (traceable and handover-ready). Any per-unit bespoke design collapses at scale.

Three standard packages#

PackagePositionContents
BasicDelivery assuranceWhole-home Wi-Fi, smart switches, lock, video doorbell, gas and water alarms
ComfortSales featureElectric curtains, dimming, scene panels, fresh air and AC integration
FlagshipPremium product lineWired dimming, whole-home sensors, AV and shading, community service integration

Principle: lock models and points in the standard packages; allow changes only in options.

Cost and selection strategy#

  • Tier the hardware: quality experience in public areas, cost efficiency elsewhere.
  • Rough-in first: neutral wires, network cable and curtain power are low cost, high value.
  • Reduce SKUs: fewer models means simpler spares and support.
  • Leave upgrade headroom: gateway capacity and spare points for owner additions.

Batch delivery workflow#

  1. Standard point plans and configuration templates per unit type.
  2. Handover briefing plus spot checks (one to two units per building fully inspected).
  3. Batch pairing and commissioning from templates to reduce human variance.
  4. Per-unit acceptance: scene response, offline test, device online rate.
  5. Documentation: device list, acceptance report, owner manual, property handover sheet.

Acceptance criteria (put in the contract)#

  • Scene response: panel ≤ 0.5 s, voice ≤ 1.5 s.
  • Network: ≥ −65 dBm in every room, zero dropouts in 24 hours.
  • Offline capability: lighting, curtains and panels work without internet.
  • First-time pass rate ≥ 95%, rectification closed within 7 days.

Show units and sales tools#

  • Four demonstrable scenes per show unit with a 90-second explanation board.
  • Sales scripts and owner handbooks to keep promises consistent.
  • Use real delivery data (online rate, response time) as marketing material.

The most overlooked step is consistent briefing. Turn installation standards into an illustrated manual so the work is copied rather than remembered.

Still have questions?

What does developer smart home cost?

Basic packages usually run ¥60–150 per m² of floor area, comfort ¥150–350 per m², flagship from ¥400 per m². Circuit count, panel material and wired dimming are the main variables.

What if a buyer does not want it?

Keep the rough-in and deliver mechanical switches. The cost increment is minimal and the upgrade path is preserved.

How does property management maintain it?

We provide management-company access, device ledger templates and remote diagnostics. On-site staff handle restarts and ticket dispatch.
