Integrators: turn delivery into an assembly line

Integrator profit does not come from charging more. It comes from the same project using two fewer days of labour and three fewer site visits.

Efficiency comes from four assets: templated solutions, replicable configuration, remote diagnostics, and spares and supply chain.

Technical foundation: consistency beats novelty#

  • Network layer: CAT6A, PoE and VLAN standards applied to every project.
  • Control layer: commit to one or two hub ecosystems and go deep.
  • Interaction layer: panels and voice vary by budget, but interfaces stay constant.
  • Local first: critical scenes execute locally; the cloud handles remote access and data.

Reusable assets#

AssetEffect
Point plan templatesLess design time per project
Selection libraryModel, protocol, compatibility and price in one place
Scene configuration packsImport per unit type instead of building on site
Acceptance checklistsConsistent delivery, fewer call-backs
Fault knowledge baseNew engineers get productive faster

Remote operations: turn visits into tickets#

  • Central monitoring of device online rates and gateway status with alerting.
  • Remote restart, parameter push and scene updates where devices support it.
  • Customer scans a code, a ticket is raised, resolved remotely or dispatched — all logged.
  • Typical result: 30–50% fewer truck rolls.

Supply chain and spares#

  • Standard spares: gateways, panels, sensors, motors, PoE switches.
  • Safety stock sized to project volume.
  • Keep 3–5% spare units per critical model to avoid second visits.

Engineer training#

  • Foundation: networking, protocols, points, installation standards.
  • Practical: commissioning workflow, scene design, fault diagnosis.
  • Certification tiers tied to dispatch rights.

Turn every service call into a template update. A mature integrator has a growing fault library and a falling truck-roll rate.

Can we partner on just part of this?

Yes. Many partners use only our templates, remote operations platform or supply chain, or collaborate on one specific unit-type series.

Does remote operations need extra hardware?

Usually not, provided hubs and network devices support a remote channel. We provide platform accounts and onboarding configuration.

Which brands are supported?

Open standards first: Zigbee/Thread/Matter devices, plus equipment with local APIs and common bus protocols (RS485, Modbus, dry contact).
