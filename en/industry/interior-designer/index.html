Interior designers: make smart living part of the design

The designer's pain is that beautiful renders arrive on site with corners cut. Smart home is where most corners get cut — unless points and selections are locked at design stage.

The best collaboration model: produce the point plan and lighting circuit table alongside the design, treat smart panels as part of material selection, and use a scene table instead of verbal instructions.

Three documents to produce together#

  1. Point plan — switches, scene panels, sensors, curtain motors, APs and network points.
  2. Lighting circuit table — which fixture belongs to which circuit, dimmable or not, target colour temperature and illuminance.
  3. Scene table — per space: scene name, trigger and lighting ratios.

A render without a circuit table is the number one killer of lighting design.

Panels are part of the material palette#

  • Consider panel colour and finish alongside walls, joinery and switches — do not default to white plastic.
  • Bezel dimensions affect cut-outs, so confirm during joinery detailing.
  • Keep one panel series per space to avoid visual noise.
  • Glass and sintered stone look excellent but lack tactile travel; brief older or new users.

Lighting coordination essentials#

  • Unified colour temperature within each open space.
  • Match dimming method to drivers — 0-10V, DALI, phase-cut or wireless.
  • LED strips in aluminium channels with diffusers.
  • Separate ambient, accent and mood layers so each can be controlled.

Show the effect, do not describe it#

  • Present a scene table with renders at different colour temperatures and illuminance.
  • Walk the client through arrive → movie → sleep in a show flat; conversion improves noticeably.
  • Hand over the scene list as a deliverable — clients understand it and it makes later adjustments easy.

Still have questions?

Can the design fee include the smart home scope?

Yes. A common model is the designer charging for smart schematic design and coordination while implementation and commissioning are delivered by the specialist.

What if we have no smart home experience?

We supply point plan templates, circuit tables and selection handbooks, and support the design-stage coordination directly.

How should a limited budget be handled?

Protect lighting circuits and network rough-in first (they cannot be redone), phase the sensors and automations, and focus the visible quality on lighting and panel finish.
