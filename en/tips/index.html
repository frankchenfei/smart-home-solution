Still have questions?

Are more automations better?

No. Eight to fifteen core automations per household is the comfortable range; beyond 25 they start conflicting and become hard to debug. Fewer and stable beats many and chaotic.

What if elderly family members cannot use it?

Keep physical switches, and give them a few large one-touch scenes (sleep, night, arrive). Voice should be an aid, never the only entry point.
