Direct-operation cities (6)#

Shenzhen (headquarters), Shanghai, Beijing, Hangzhou, Chengdu and Suzhou are delivered by our own teams, with one delivery standard and one acceptance checklist. Every city team has the same roles: solution designer, site supervisor, commissioning engineer and after-sales support.

Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan#

These regions use remote design review, certified local partners for installation, and our engineers attending the critical milestones. Cross-border logistics, estate or building notifications and site coordination are handled jointly.

How we serve other cities#

  • Remote design review — send floor plans and site photos; we produce the point plan, device list and scene table.
  • Certified partner delivery — local teams we train and certify, backed by our engineering support and acceptance standards.
  • Key project support — engineers travel for large flats, villas, show units and commercial projects.

Wherever the project is, delivery is complete only when all four documents exist: point plan, scene table, acceptance sheet and user manual.

Still have questions?

Is the on-site survey free?

The first survey is free within 30 km of the city centre in our direct-operation cities. Cross-border and outlying projects are quoted at travel cost, deductible from the contract.

How do you serve Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan?

Through remote design review, certified local partners for installation and our engineers attending the critical milestones. We produce the point plan, scene table and configuration templates, the partner handles site work and making good, and we verify the electrical briefing and commissioning milestones remotely or on site.

How do you guarantee quality outside your cities?

Three measures: head-office review of the point plan, remote or on-site acceptance at electrical handover and commissioning, and central archiving of delivery documents.
