On-site surveys and handovers are available across these districts; outlying areas are served via remote design review plus attendance at key milestones.

What is different in Taiwan#

Retrofits in Taiwan are usually done without disturbing existing finishes: smart fixtures, wireless panels, battery curtain motors and Zigbee or Thread sensors are the most popular combination. Older two-wire switch circuits and aluminium wiring are common, so we assess electrical safety during the survey before deciding the smart scheme.

Our delivery process in Taiwan#

  1. Survey — measure, photograph, and assess the existing power and weak-current installation.
  2. Design — point plan, device list, scene table and quotation within three working days.
  3. Handover — electrical and carpentry briefings together with your builder and designer.
  4. Installation — device fitting and circuit checks to standard.
  5. Commissioning — scene-by-scene testing, network tuning, offline test.
  6. Handover — user training plus four documents.
  7. After-sales — local support backed by remote diagnostics, with response times in the contract.

Delivery standard (identical nationwide)#

  • Scene response: panel ≤ 0.5 s, voice ≤ 1.5 s.
  • Every room ≥ −65 dBm with no dropouts in 24 hours.
  • Lighting, curtains and panels remain controllable without internet.
  • 30 days of free tuning, with on-site response during the warranty period.

Ask us to arrange a show-flat visit during the design stage. Seeing the experience before committing is the best way to choose a scheme.

Still have questions?

How far ahead should I book a survey in Taiwan?

Standard lead time is three to seven days; for an electrical handover milestone book at least ten days ahead so we can attend with your builder. Peak seasons (March–May and September–November) need about a week more.

Is pricing the same as other cities?

Device and labour rates are national, but delivery cost varies with floor level, ceiling height and distance from the city centre. Every line is itemised in the quotation.

Can you handle projects in other cities?

Yes. Head office produces and reviews the point plan, certified local partners install, and we provide engineering support, milestone acceptance and document archiving.
