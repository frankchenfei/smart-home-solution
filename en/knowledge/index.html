Still have questions?

Must a smart home be installed during renovation?

No, but renovation is the cheapest window. Cabling, neutral wires, curtain power and AP locations are almost impossible to add cheaply later; devices themselves can always be added.

How many network cables does a 100 m² home need?

Typically 8–14: 2–3 per TV location, 1 per AP location, 2 for a study, 2 from the service box to the main router, plus 1–2 for the door camera and lock gateway. A cable costs very little; retrofit costs far more.
