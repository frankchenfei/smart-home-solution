What we do#

SmartHaus is operated by Shenzhen Youlang Industrial Co., Ltd., with offices and warehousing at G2-117 Yufengyuan, No.108 Fenghuang Avenue, Pinghu Street, Longgang District, Shenzhen. For homeowners we design, install, commission and support whole-home smart systems. For businesses — renovation companies, designers, developers, hotel and apartment operators, integrators and retailers — we provide standardised solutions, supply chain and delivery systems.

Our method#

  1. Rough-in first — everything buried in a wall must be right the first time.
  2. Local first — critical scenes run locally: faster, offline-capable, more private.
  3. Verifiable — every project hands over a point plan, scene table, acceptance sheet and user manual.
  4. No lock-in — critical devices on open standards, two hub paths, accounts owned by the client.

Why we publish our knowledge#

We publish the pitfalls, the pricing logic and the acceptance criteria because opacity is the industry's biggest cost. You can only choose well if you can understand.

The team#

Solution designers, site supervisors, commissioning engineers and after-sales support — all in house. Engineers hold low-voltage electrical qualifications and commissioning engineers are internally certified in scene design.

We do not represent a single brand. Selection prioritises protocol openness and local control capability.

Still have questions?

Do you only sell devices?

No. We deliver design, installation, commissioning and support, or design and technical briefing only if that is what you need.

Are you worried competitors copy your published guides?

Not really. A more transparent industry makes clients more willing to pay for expertise, which benefits everyone.
