Money#

Q: How much does a whole-home smart system cost? For 100–140 m²: entry ¥15,000–30,000, practical ¥40,000–80,000, premium ¥100,000–250,000. The spread comes from dimming method, lighting circuit count, panel materials and whether commissioning is included. Anything under ¥10,000 is a few switches and a gateway, not a whole-home system.

Q: Why do quotes differ threefold? Four variables: dimming method, circuit count, panel materials, and whether commissioning and after-sales are included. A quote that does not itemise these cannot be compared.

Q: Can I start with part of it? Yes, and we recommend it: rough-in, network and lighting circuits during construction, sensors and automations after you move in.

Timing and process#

Q: When must decisions be locked in? Before the electrical handover. Neutral wires, curtain power, network points and service box size are extremely expensive to change once the walls close.

Q: How long does delivery take? For 100–140 m²: one day surveying, three working days for the design, two to four days installing, one to two days commissioning. Integrated with a renovation, the work concentrates on two milestones.

Q: Can my builder do it? Possibly — check three things: a point plan, an acceptance-tested scene table, and clarity on who owns after-sales.

Brands and protocols#

Q: Do I need a big brand? Not necessarily. Judge on Matter support or an open local API, offline behaviour and ecosystem interoperability. Established brands are usually more reliable in service, which is not the same as requiring a single brand throughout.

Q: Will I be locked in? If every device only works in one app with no local interface, the risk is real. Mitigate by using open standards for critical devices, keeping two hub paths, and putting ownership in the contract.

Q: Zigbee, Thread, Matter or Wi-Fi? Wired backbone (Ethernet/RS485), Zigbee or Thread for sensors and control, Wi-Fi for a few high-bandwidth devices. Matter standardises the application layer but does not change the underlying transport.

Q: Does it work without internet? It depends on the hub. With a local-first architecture, lighting, curtains, panels and most automations still work. Cloud-only systems stop entirely. Always test offline at acceptance.

Installation details#

Q: My old home has no neutral wires. What now? Preference order: re-run neutrals, then smart fixtures with wireless panels, then two-wire smart switches (with minimum load limits and flicker risk).

Q: How much network cable do I need? Typically 8–14 runs for 100 m². Cable is cheap; retrofitting is not.

Q: Will a smart switch work with my existing fixtures? Usually, with two boundaries: no motor or high-capacitance loads, and respect the minimum load. Send the fixture specification to your provider first.

Q: What about bathrooms and balconies with no signal? Cover from the corridor AP, add a wall AP where needed, and convert balcony cameras to wired PoE. Test every room rather than the living room alone.

Support and longevity#

Q: Who repairs it? We provide in-warranty device and system support with contracted response times. Around 60% of issues are resolved remotely (restarts, re-pairing, parameter changes); the rest are site visits.

Q: How long do devices last? Panels and sensors 5–8 years; curtain motors 5–8 years and best treated as consumables with an access hatch planned; hubs and network gear are typically refreshed around five years. Systems can be upgraded device by device.

Q: Can I take it with me when I move? Devices on open standards or re-pairable ones can move; built-in switches depend on the model. Second-hand devices usually require the previous owner to unpair them first.

Q: Do you only work in Shanghai? We operate directly in Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, Hangzhou, Chengdu and Suzhou. Elsewhere we deliver via remote design review and certified partners, with project teams travelling for key projects.
